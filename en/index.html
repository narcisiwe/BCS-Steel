Metal. Ideas.
Working together.

BCS Steel specializes in metal processing and custom metal fabrication. We offer laser cutting, sheet metal bending, TIG and laser welding, as well as the manufacture of stainless steel structures and products for the food, pharmaceutical and other industrial sectors, and for private customers.

We begin every project by assessing the customer’s requirements. We work with completed technical drawings as well as customer sketches or ideas. When needed, we prepare technical drawings and develop a structural solution, then manufacture the finished product.

  1. Your drawing or sketch
  2. Agreeing the solution
  3. Manufacturing

You can send a completed technical drawing, a sketch, or simply describe your idea. If needed, we will prepare the technical drawing and propose a suitable manufacturing solution.
